# Bioinstrumentation-Project-Oximeter
The blood oxygen & heart rate monitor is a
simple and non-invasive device that measures
blood oxygen levels (SpO2) and heart rate. It
works by shining red and infrared light
through the skin to detect how much light is
absorbed by oxygen-rich and oxygen-poor
blood. This helps in checking lung and heart
health.
 These devices are widely used in hospitals,
home healthcare, and fitness monitoring. With
advancements in technology, combining them
with Arduino provides an affordable and
effective way to track health in real time even
at homes
